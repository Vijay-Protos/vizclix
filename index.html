From Enquiry to Delivery — Step by Step.

Most of a good aerial shoot is decided before take-off. Timings are confirmed per project.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the property, the location and what the footage is for. We reply with questions and a scope.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Site, airspace and permissions checked. Shot list set around light and weather.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    Flights to the shot list, with extra coverage where the brief calls for it.

  4. 04

    Edit & Grade

    Selection, cut and colour matched to the property and its audience.
